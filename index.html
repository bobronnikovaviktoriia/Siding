What we install

Materials for every budget and climate

We help you pick the siding that fits your home, your budget, and how it needs to hold up against Pacific Northwest rain and wind.

Vinyl Siding

The most affordable option. Won't rot, never needs painting, and comes in a wide range of colors and textures.

Metal Siding

Holds up well against cold, wind, and rain. A solid choice for houses, cabins, and outbuildings alike.

Fiber Cement Siding

Fire-resistant and dense, with a wood- or stone-look finish. Built to last for decades with minimal upkeep.

Wood Siding / Log Siding

A natural, warm look for the exterior. We install with proper sealing and protective treatment against moisture.

Foundation Siding

Stone- and brick-look panels for the foundation — protects the base of the house from moisture and impact.

Trim & Insulation

We can also select and install insulation, weather barriers, trim, and all the finishing accessories, start to finish.
